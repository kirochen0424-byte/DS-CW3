📼 The Digital Archive: DS-CW3
A "Modern-Retro" Legacy Project for Andy's Digital Sandbox.
Finally, it's alive! After a long battle with terminal commands, port conflicts, and university firewalls, this archive is officially open for business. It's a full-stack digital legacy quiz that asks you one question nobody really wants to think about: what happens to your online presence after you're gone?

🌍 Live Demo
Check out the archive here: 👉 https://ds-cw3.onrender.com
Pro tip: I'm on a free Render server, so the site might take 30-50 seconds to wake up on first load. Think of it as a vintage computer warming up its vacuum tubes. Worth the wait, probably.

🛠️ What's Under the Hood?
Brain: Node.js & Express
Vault: MongoDB Atlas (Cloud Database)
Home: Render (bypassing the legendary Goldsmiths firewall)
Shipment: Git & GitHub

📝 A Quick Note
The retro terminal look is intentional. It's not a bug, it's a vibe. The quiz is also fully wired to a live database, so your answers actually get saved. Yes, really.
